Privacy policy

Family Dashboard · Updated October 26, 2026

Family Dashboard is a private, noncommercial household application operated by Brian. This policy describes its Google Calendar and Google Health connections. Support, disconnection, and deletion requests: contact Brian directly through your existing household contact, or use the user support email shown on the Google authorization screen.

Information accessed and purpose

Google Calendar: read-only access lists available calendars during setup and reads the selected calendar's metadata and events to display a household agenda. Returned event data can include event titles, dates, times, descriptions, locations, and other fields supplied by Google. The dashboard does not create, edit, or delete Google Calendar events.

Google Health: a separate read-only sleep permission reads the account's Health identity during setup and reconciled recent sleep sessions. Displayed summaries include the sleep date, bedtime, wake time, time asleep, and available Light, Deep, REM, and Awake stage totals. The feature does not request Google activity or broader health-measurement permissions.

Sharing and visibility

Authorized household users and people viewing shared household screens can see displayed agenda and sleep information. The Mac operator can administer the application. Account owners must agree to that household visibility before connecting. Remote dashboard access uses Tailscale and an allowed-user list.

The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, send it to AI services, or use it to train AI models. The public information website does not connect to the private dashboard or publish household records. Google and Tailscale process requests and connection information under their own service policies.

Storage and security

OAuth client credentials and refresh tokens are stored in an owner-only configuration file on the household Mac. Short-lived access tokens and recent provider responses or display summaries are held in server memory. Health identifiers and tokens are not returned to dashboard browsers. The integration does not intentionally write calendar or sleep history to disk. Displayed data can remain in memory during an outage and is labeled stale.

Browser displays, operating-system backups, and provider accounts have their own retention behavior. Configuration backups may retain credentials. Temporary local OAuth cookies expire after ten minutes or are cleared after connection.

Revocation and deletion

Revoke Calendar or Health access through your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Ask the operator to remove the relevant stored credentials and clear cached provider data. Google Health also has a Disconnect control in local setup. Close or reload open dashboard screens to remove information already displayed. Ask the operator to address retained configuration backups when requesting deletion. Removing this connection does not delete events or health records from Google.

Limited use

Family Dashboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements. Google Health data use also complies with the Google Health API Developer and User Data Policy, including its restrictions on limited use.

Other features and changes

Optional Oura, Notion, and Nest connections remain separate integrations. The dashboard's existing Oura privacy disclosures also apply to its Oura feature. This page describes Google Calendar and Google Health use; expanding the data used or its household visibility requires updated disclosures and any necessary renewed consent before the change.
