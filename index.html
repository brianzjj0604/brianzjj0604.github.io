Family Dashboard

A private, noncommercial dashboard for an invited household.

Family Dashboard brings a household agenda, lists, trackers, optional sleep summaries, and optional thermostat controls into one shared display. The application runs on a household Mac; access is limited to authorized household users.

Google connections

With the account owner's permission, the dashboard uses read-only Google Calendar access to show the selected calendar's events. A separate read-only Google Health connection shows the latest eligible main sleep session, including its timing, duration, and available sleep stages.

Google Health summaries are wellness information and are not medical advice. Connecting an account makes the displayed information visible to authorized household users and shared household screens.

About this website

This public website explains the application and its data practices. It contains no household calendar events, health records, sign-in credentials, or access to the private dashboard.
